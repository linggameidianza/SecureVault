6 reasons your data is safer in SecureVault

01

Can't be captured

Screenshots & screen recording are blocked by the system (FLAG_SECURE). App content is also hidden in the Recent Apps list.

Screen always protected
02

Data-breach check

Integrated with Have I Been Pwned: check a password, audit all stored passwords, and check whether an email has been breached.

Passwords are never sent
03

Expiring-password reminders

Set a validity of 30–365 days. Automatic notification 2 days before expiry, plus orange/red markers in the account list.

Checked every 6 hours in the background
04

AES-256 encryption

Passwords & notes are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using an Android Keystore key, and the whole database is encrypted with SQLCipher — keys never leave the device.

AES-256-GCM · SQLCipher
05

Biometric / device lock

Unlock with a strong biometric or your device screen lock. Unlocking releases the auth-bound vault key — a cryptographic lock, not just a UI gate. Auto-locks 30 seconds in the background.

Auto-lock in 30 s
06

Encrypted backup & restore

All accounts, notes & tasks in a single password-locked .svbk file (PBKDF2, 210,000 iterations). Easy to move to a new phone.

Merge / replace all

How the breach check works

Passwords are checked without ever being sent

SecureVault uses the k-Anonymity method against the Pwned Passwords database from Have I Been Pwned. The final match happens on your phone.

1

Password on phone

The password you want to check stays on the device.

P@ssw0rd123
2

SHA-1 hash

Turned into an irreversible 40-character hash.

0F0D9…E7C5F
3

Send 5 characters

Only the first 5 characters of the hash are sent to the HIBP API.

/range/0F0D9
4

Match locally

HIBP returns hundreds of similar hashes; the phone matches them itself.

match? → breach count
The HIBP server never learns your password. Email checks use an HIBP API key that is stored encrypted.
